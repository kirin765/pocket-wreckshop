# Device art references

All current device sprites are original procedural pixel art, produced by `Tools/generate_device_sprites.py`. Each of the seven families has ten aligned frames. The code draws clean casings, flat displays, boards, sealed power modules and separated components; no photographic, burnt, swollen or organic textures are used.

Recognizable shapes were informed by real hardware:

- Phone: iPhone 16-like rounded flat frame and front camera island. [Apple specifications](https://www.apple.com/iphone-16/specs/)
- Tablet: iPad Air-like rectangular all-screen body and even bezels. [Apple iPad Air](https://www.apple.com/shop/buy-ipad/ipad-air)
- Headphones: over-ear cups, metal yokes and a canopy headband, using AirPods Max-like proportions.
- Console and controller: PS5-like flared side plates, black center, and DualSense-like grips, touchpad and twin sticks. [Sony design story](https://www.sony.com/en/SonyInfo/design/stories/PS5/), [DualSense](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/accessories/dualsense-wireless-controller/)
- Speaker: rounded portable speaker body, perforated grille and visible controls.
- Monitor: thin-bezel rectangular panel and a silver desktop stand.

These are simplified game silhouettes, with generic catalogue names and no copied product artwork or logos. The earlier photographic phone sheet has been removed from the project.
